Getting Started

Welcome to PRICE IS THE KING! Follow these steps to begin monitoring your Chartink scans:

1. Create an Account

Sign up for a free account to get started.

2. Connect Chartink

Link your Chartink account or add scan URLs.

3. Configure Settings

Customize your dashboard and alerts.

4. Start Monitoring

Track your scans in real-time.

Frequently Asked Questions

To add a new scan condition:

  1. Click on the "Add Condition" button in your dashboard
  2. Enter a name for your condition
  3. Paste your Chartink scan URL or manually enter the scan parameters
  4. Click "Save" to add the condition to your dashboard

Data is refreshed automatically every 2-3 minutes. You can also manually refresh the data at any time using the refresh button in the navigation bar.
