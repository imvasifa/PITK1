Adding a New Condition

  1. Click the Add Condition button
  2. Enter a descriptive name for your condition
  3. Paste your Chartink scan URL or manually enter the scan clause
  4. Click Save to add the condition
  5. Your condition will now appear in the dashboard

Managing Your Conditions

You can manage your conditions using the following actions:

  • Edit: Click the icon to modify a condition
  • Delete: Click the icon to remove a condition
  • Refresh: Click the refresh button available in the Navigation bat at the top to manually update all conditions

Using Chartink Screens

To use CHARTINK screens with this tool:

  1. Go to chartink.com and log in
  2. Create or open a scanner with your desired conditions
  3. Click on "Copy Link" or copy the URL from your browser
  4. Paste this URL when adding a new condition

Auto Refresh Feature

The dashboard automatically refreshes every 2 minutes to show the latest data. You can also manually refresh at any time by clicking the refresh button in the navigation bar. Inside the dashboard option you can select the time interval.

Nifty Indices Information

Real-time Data from NSE India

Our Nifty Indices data is fetched in real-time directly from the National Stock Exchange (NSE) of India. The percentage changes shown are calculated from the Day's Open price, not the previous day's close. This gives you a more accurate view of the current day's market movement.

Key points about Nifty Indices data:

  • Data is updated every 30 seconds during market hours
  • All values are in real-time with minimal delay
  • Percentage change is calculated from the day's opening price
  • Data is sourced directly from NSE India's official feed
