About This App

This application works on top of Chartink's scanning platform. It allows you to monitor and manage your Chartink scans collectively. To use this app, you'll need to first create your scan conditions on Chartink.com and then add them here using the scan URL or scan clause.

Adding a New Condition

  1. Click the Add Condition button
  2. Enter a descriptive name for your condition
  3. Paste your Chartink scan URL or manually enter the scan clause
  4. Click Save to add the condition
  5. Your condition will now appear in the dashboard

Managing Your Conditions

You can manage your conditions using the following actions:

  • Edit: Click the icon to modify a condition
  • Delete: Click the icon to remove a condition
  • Refresh: Click the refresh button in the navigation bar to manually update all conditions

Using Chartink Screens

To use CHARTINK screens with this tool:

  1. Go to chartink.com and log in
  2. Create or open a scanner with your desired conditions
  3. Click on "Copy Link" or copy the URL from your browser
  4. Paste this URL when adding a new condition

Auto Refresh Feature

The dashboard automatically refreshes to show the latest data. You can also manually refresh at any time by clicking the refresh button in the navigation bar. Inside the dashboard option you can select the time interval.

Light/Dark Theme

You can switch between light and dark themes using the theme toggle button in the navigation bar. Your preference will be saved for future visits.
